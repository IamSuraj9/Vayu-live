Why these choices
  • Streamlines with arrowheads and curved barbs are read more accurately than classic straight barbs. Ware et al., BAMS 2014
  • Weather charts draw isobars every 4 hPa from 1000, and mark highs and lows with H and L. NOAA JetStream
  • A wind barb's staff points to where the wind comes from; each long feather is 10 knots, a flag is 50. NWS Honolulu
  • Rainbow scales mislead because equal steps do not look equal, but a well-designed one can still work. Google Turbo, Ware 2023
